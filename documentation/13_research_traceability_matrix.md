# End-to-End Research Traceability Matrix

The **Research Traceability Matrix** maps every pipeline stage, script, input dependency, output artifact, next consumer stage, and ultimate contribution to the final language model benchmarking results.

---

| Stage # | Script Path | Input Artifacts & Files | Output Artifacts Generated | Next Consumer Stage | Final Research & Benchmarking Contribution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | `scripts/01_parse_dictionary.py` | `data/Data Dictionary...csv` | `outputs/dictionary_metadata.json` | Stage 04, Stage 10 | Establishes field categories, data types, and bidirectional ID-to-Display column mappings for 142 attributes. |
| **02** | `scripts/02_profile_dataset.py` | Raw CSV files in `data/*.csv` | `outputs/profiling_report.json` | Stage 03 | Quantifies row counts, null counts, cardinality ratios, primary key candidates, and inferred foreign keys. |
| **03** | `scripts/03_discover_relationships.py` | `outputs/profiling_report.json` | `outputs/relationships.json` | Data validation & architecture | Constructs NetworkX directed relational graph ($G = (V, E)$) establishing parent-child join directions. |
| **04** | `scripts/04_select_semantic_columns.py` | `outputs/dictionary_metadata.json` | `outputs/selected_semantic_columns.json` | Stage 05 | Filters administrative metadata and French columns, retaining 83 high-value semantic NLP columns. |
| **05** | `scripts/05_merge_tables.py` | Raw CSVs, `selected_semantic_columns.json` | `outputs/merged_records.jsonl`, `merge_reconciliation_report.json` | Stage 05a, Stage 06 | Executes relational left joins on composite key `(VesselID, OccID)`, preventing Cartesian joins and handling orphans. |
| **05a** | `scripts/05a_validate_records.py` | `outputs/merged_records.jsonl`, Raw CSVs | `outputs/validation_report.json` | Stage 09, Quality auditing | Validates physical metric bounds (speed, tonnage, crew, temp), date plausibility, and primary key integrity. |
| **06** | `scripts/06_generate_documents.py` | `outputs/merged_records.jsonl` | `outputs/raw_documents.jsonl` | Stage 07 | Synthesizes 63,730 operational narratives using 5 template families, Knowledge Unit Graph, and Concept Gain Calculator. |
| **07** | `scripts/07_clean_documents.py` | `outputs/raw_documents.jsonl` | `outputs/clean_documents.jsonl` | Stage 08, 09, 10, 11, 12, 18 | Cleans text, splits sentences with abbreviation lookbehinds, deduplicates sentences, and executes MD5 cross-document deduplication (42,150 final docs). |
| **08** | `scripts/08_export_corpus.py` | `outputs/clean_documents.jsonl` | `outputs/maritime_corpus.txt`, `maritime_corpus.jsonl`, `manifest.json` | Downstream pretraining | Exports dual-format pretraining corpus (plain text & metadata-preserving JSONL) and embeds git commit provenance. |
| **09** | `scripts/09_statistics.py` | `outputs/clean_documents.jsonl`, Reports | `outputs/statistics.json`, `corpus_quality_report.md` | Research reports, Stage 11 | Computes Shannon Entropy, length percentiles, MID density, and scaffold-reduced MinHash LSH near-duplication rate. |
| **10** | `scripts/10_extract_vocabulary.py` | `outputs/clean_documents.jsonl`, Metadata | `outputs/maritime_vocabulary.txt` | Stage 13, Stage 14 | Extracts top single-word and multi-word maritime domain terms (`restricted visibility`, `propulsion failure`). |
| **11** | `scripts/11_corpus_representations.py` | `outputs/clean_documents.jsonl` | `outputs/corpus_representations/*.jsonl` (5 files) | Stage 14 | Generates 5 multi-format corpus representations (Narrative, Key-Value, Template, JSON, Mixed) to test representation robustness. |
| **12** | `scripts/12_semantic_importance.py` | `outputs/clean_documents.jsonl` | `outputs/document_importance.jsonl`, `subsets/*.jsonl` (6 files) | Stage 14 | Scores document importance via 10 features ($0 \le S \le 100$), classifies Knowledge Tiers, and generates 6 evaluation subsets. |
| **13** | `scripts/13_tokenizer_analysis.py` | `outputs/maritime_vocabulary.txt`, Clean docs | `outputs/tokenizer_analysis/*.json`, `tokenizer_comparison.csv` | Stage 15, Stage 17 | Profiles 14 Hugging Face tokenizers across single-token coverage %, fertility, fragmentation %, OOV %, and speed. |
| **14** | `scripts/14_mlm_evaluation.py` | Reps, Subsets, Vocabulary, 7 HF Models | `outputs/evaluations/cache/*.json` (350 files) | Stage 15 | Executes 350-run Matrix MLM Evaluation Grid measuring MLM loss, Top-1/5/10 accuracy, and 6 subdomain category recalls. |
| **15** | `scripts/15_cross_model_benchmarking.py` | Evaluation cache files, Tokenizer reports | `outputs/comparison.csv`, `leaderboard.csv`, `visualizations/*.png` | Stage 16, Stage 17 | Computes **Maritime Understanding Index (MUI)** composite scores, 95% CIs, ranks leaderboard, and generates publication plots. |
| **16** | `scripts/16_statistical_analysis.py` | `outputs/comparison.csv` | `outputs/statistical_significance.json`, `ablation_study.json` | Stage 17, Appendix B | Computes paired t-tests, Wilcoxon $p$-values, Cohen's d, Cliff's Delta, Bootstrap CIs, and scoring engine feature ablation drops. |
| **17** | `scripts/17_decision_engine.py` | `outputs/leaderboard.csv`, Config thresholds | `outputs/experiment_metadata.json`, `decision_summary.json`, `benchmark_report.md` | Final Pretraining Prescription | Executes programmatic decision engine, threshold sensitivity analysis, captures environment metadata, and writes 10-section report. |
| **18** | `scripts/18_lint_corpus.py` | `outputs/clean_documents.jsonl` | `outputs/corpus_lint_report.json` | Quality Gate Audit | Evaluates automated regex quality linter across 5 rules, enforcing zero-defect corpus quality pass (`PASS` status). |
